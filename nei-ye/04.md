## 內業 Nei ye IV

### [IV.1]

凡道無所
It is ever so that the Way has no fixed place, (所 *·siay*)[^nei-ye-76]

[^nei-ye-76]: Both the Yang and Zhao editions break the text beginning with this line to form a new section.

善心安處[^nei-ye-77]
Yet in a good mind it will peacefully settle. (處 *t’jay*)

[^nei-ye-77]: Emending 愛 (*·ər*) to 處 (*t’jay*) to complete the rhyme with (所 *·siay*), above
[Wang Niansun, Tao Hongqing, and Guo Moruo].

心靜
The mind quiescent,
氣理
and the vital force well managed, (理 *liəy*)

道乃可止
The Way can then be made to stay. (止 *tiəy*)

### [IV.2]

彼道不遠
The Way is never far removed. (遠 *yjwan*)

民得以生[^nei-ye-78]
By obtaining it, people may live. (生 *srian*)

[^nei-ye-78]: Reading 產 as 生 [Ma Feibai].
[Also emending 產 to 生 as the rhyme character, but not the rhyme, which is *sreng* elsewhere --RSE.]

彼道不離
The Way is never detached. (離 *lia*)

民因以和[^nei-ye-79]
By relying on it, people become harmonious. (和 *gwa*)

[^nei-ye-79]: Following Guo Moruo in emending 知 (*tiey*), “knowledge” or “ability to know,”
to 和 (*gwa*) to complete the rhyme with 離 (*lia*).

是故卒乎
Thus, how immediate!
其如可與索[^nei-ye-80]
As if one were bound together with it. (索 *sak*)

[^nei-ye-80]: Rhyme: 索 (group 魚, 4th tone) 所 (group 魚, 2nd tone), below.

眇眇乎
How distant!
其如窮無所
As if it were exhausting infinity. (所 *·siay*)

### [IV.3]

彼[^nei-ye-81]道之情
That Way’s inner reality, (情 *dzieng*)

[^nei-ye-81]: The Yang edition mistakenly writes 被 for 彼.

惡音與聲
Rejects sound and speech. (聲 *·stjieng*)

脩心靜意[^nei-ye-82]
Only after cultivating one’s mind and quieting one’s power of awareness, (意 *·iəy*)

[^nei-ye-82]: Rhyme: 意 (之 group, 3rd tone) with 得 (group 之, 4th tone), below.

道乃可得
May the Way be comprehended. (得 *təy*)
