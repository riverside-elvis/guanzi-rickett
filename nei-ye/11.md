## 內業 Nei ye XI

### [XI.1]

摶[^nei-ye-135]氣如神
If you concentrate your vital force until you become like the Spirit, (神 *zdjien*)[^nei-ye-136]

[^nei-ye-135]: The Yang and the Zhao (Ming print) editions, both here and in the following line,
mistakenly write 撙 for 摶 = 專 [Liu Ji,Wang Niansun, Yasui,and Guo Moruo].

[^nei-ye-136]: This portion of stanza XI is similar in content to the “Xin shu xia,” 6a9-6b3 (66.13-67.2).

萬物備存[^nei-ye-137]
Your grasp of all things will be complete. (存 *dzwən*)

[^nei-ye-137]: Irregular rhyme: 神 (group 其) with 存 (group 文).

能摶乎
Can you concentrate your mind?
能一[^nei-ye-138]乎
Can you focus your power of awareness? (一 *·jiet*)

[^nei-ye-138]: Again, the meaning of 一 presents a problem.
I have read it in accordance with a statement farther along in this stanza (5a8; 103.3): 一意搏(專)心,
“You will focus (i.e., unify) your power of awareness and concentrate your mind.”
Such an interpretation is further supported by somewhat similar passages in the “Xin shu xia” 6a9-10 (66.13)
and the “Shun dao” 艰道 section of the Mawangdui _Shi da jing_ (_Shiliu jing_), 88:
“Can you focus? Can you desist? Can you rise above yourself? Can you make decisions on your own
while honoring the inherent order of things?” However, in the _Zhuangzi_,
VIII, 23/4b7-10 (Watson, _Chuang Tzu_, 253), there is another passage very similar in content
that contains the phrase 錶抱一乎. In this case, 一 is clearly a noun and refers to “the One”
(perhaps the Way or perhaps true human nature or the undivided state underlying one’s consciousness).
The _Zhuangzi_ passage reads: “Laozi said, ‘The cardinal precept in preserving life is---Can
you embrace the One and never lose it? Without resorting to tortoise shell and milfoil,
can you foretell good fortune from bad? Can you tell where to stop?
Can you tell when to desist? Can you disregard it in others and seek it within yourself?”

能無卜筮
Without resorting to tortoise shell and milfoil,
而知凶吉[^nei-ye-139]乎
can you foretell bad fortune from good? (吉 *kjiet*)

[^nei-ye-139]: Inverting the two characters 吉凶 in accordance with
the parallel passage in the “Xin shu xia,” 6a11 (66.14),
and to preserve the rhyme with 一 (*·jiet*) [Wang Niansun and Yasui].

能止乎
Can you tell where to stop?
能已乎
Can you tell when to desist? (已 *riəy*)

能勿求諸人
Rather than seeking it in others,
而得[^nei-ye-140]之己乎
can you find it within yourself? (己 *kiəy*)

[^nei-ye-140]: The Yang edition mistakenly deletes 得.

思之
Think about it!
思之
Think about it! (之 *tjiəy*)

又重思之
Again think about it! (之 *tjiəy*)

思之而不通
If you still cannot fathom it,
鬼神將通之
ghosts and spirits will help you fathom it. (通 *tjiəy*)

非鬼神之力也
This is not because they possess some special prowess. (力 *liək*)

精氣之極也
It is because the vital essence and its vital force are supreme. (極 *giək*)

### [XI.2]

四體既正
The four parts of the body will be in correct order. (正 *tjieng*)

血氣既靜
Your pulse and breath will become quiet. (靜 *dzieng*)

一意摶心
You will focus your power of awareness and concentrate your mind. (心 *sjiəm*)

耳目不淫
Your ears and eyes will not be distracted, (淫 *riəm*)

雖遠
And even though things be distant,
若近[^nei-ye-141]
they will appear as if near at hand. (近 *gjən*)

[^nei-ye-141]: Irregular rhyme: 近 (group 文) with 淫 (group 侵), above.

### [XI.3]

思索生知
Thought and inquiry produce knowledge.

慢易生憂
Slackness and carelessness produce sorrow.

暴傲生怨
Violence and arrogance produce resentment.

憂鬱生疾
Sorrow and melancholy produce illness. (疾 *dzjiet*)

疾困乃死[^nei-ye-142]
Sickness and trouble bring death. (死 *sjier*)

[^nei-ye-142]: Irregular rhyme: 死 (group 脂, 2nd tone) with 疾 (group 脂, 4th tone) above.

思之而不捨
If you continue to think with no relaxation, (捨 *st’iay*)

內困
Inwardly you will be troubled,
外薄[^nei-ye-143]
and outwardly you will grow thin. (薄 *bwak*)

[^nei-ye-143]: Irregular rhyme: 薄 (group 魚, 4th tone) with 拾 (group 魚, 1st tone), above.

不蚤為圖
If you do not make early plans to prevent this, (圖 *day*)

生將巽舍
Your life will relinquish its abode. (舍 *st’iay*)

### [XI.4]

食
When eating,
莫若無飽
it is best not to overindulge.

思
When thinking,
莫若勿致
it is best not to overdo. (致 *tjier*)

節適之齊
When there is a suitable equilibrium, (齊 *dzer*)

彼將自至
Long life will naturally be achieved. (至 *tjier*)
