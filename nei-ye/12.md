## 內業 Nei ye XII

凡人之生也
It is ever so that in man’s life, (生 *sreng*)

天出其精
Heaven produces his vital essence. (精 *tsjieng*)

地出其形
Earth produces his form. (形 *geng*)

合此以為人
These combine in order to produce man.

和乃生
When they are in harmony, there is life. (生 *sreng*)

不和不生
Without it, there is no life. (生 *sreng*)

察和之道
If one searches for the way to it, (道 *dəw*)

其情[^nei-ye-144]不見
Its inner reality cannot be seen;
其徵不醜
its outward manifestation cannot be classified. (醜 *t’jəw*)

[^nei-ye-144]: Emending 精 to 情 [Yasui, Dai Wang, and Guo Moruo].

平正擅匈淪洽[^nei-ye-145]在心
However, when equanimity and good judgment fill the breast and engulf the mind,
此以長壽
this brings long life. (壽 *djəw*)

[^nei-ye-145]: Emending  論治 to 淪洽 [Guo Moruo].

窖[^nei-ye-146]怒之失度
If joy and anger are excessive,

[^nei-ye-146]: Emending 忿 to 窖 in accordance with the text below [Ding Shihan].

乃為之圖
Deal with them in a planned manner. (圖 *day*)

節其五欲
Moderate the five desires[^nei-ye-147]
去其二凶
and get rid of the two violent emotions. (凶 *xjewng*)[^nei-ye-148]

[^nei-ye-147]: The five desires stem from the action of the five organs:
ears, eyes, nose, mouth, and heart/mind,
and involve hearing, seeing, smelling, tasting, and love and hate.

[^nei-ye-148]: The two violent emotions are joy and anger.

不喜不怒
Be neither joyous nor angry,
平正擅匈
then equanimity and good judgment will fill your breast. (匈 *xjewng*)
