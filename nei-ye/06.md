## 內業 Nei ye VI

### [VI.1]

凡道
It is ever so that the Way[^nei-ye-86]

[^nei-ye-86]: Both the Yang and Zhao editions break the text with this line to begin a new section.
Ma Feibai follows suit to begin a new stanza. Riegel/Haloun and Zhao Shouzheng
would include this stanza in the preceding one. Shi Yishen believes it represents a misplaced slip
and should be inserted in line 1b10 (100.1) after 日用其德, “we may daily use its Power.”

無根無莖
Has neither roots nor stalks, (莖 *greng*)

無葉無榮
Neither leaves nor blossoms. (榮 *·iweng*)

萬物以生
Yet what to all things gives life (生 *sreng*)

萬物以成
And brings them to fruition (成 *djieng*)

命之曰道
Is termed the Way.

### [VI.2]

天主正[^nei-ye-87]
The supreme quality of Heaven lies in its regularity (正 *tjieng*)

[^nei-ye-87]: 正 here refers to the Heaven’s regularity in respect to the seasons.
Below, it refers to good judgment in people.

地主平
Of Earth, it is equity; (平 *bieng*)[^nei-ye-88]

[^nei-ye-88]: I.e., it provides support equally to all living things.

人主安靜
Of man, it is quiescence. (靜 *dzjieng*)

春秋
Spring and autumn,
冬夏
summer and winter,
天之時也
are Heaven’s seasons. (時 *diəy*)

山陵
Mountains and hills,
川谷
rivers and valleys,
地之材[^nei-ye-89]也
provide Earth’s resources. (材 *dzəy*)

[^nei-ye-89]: Emending 枝 to 材 (*dzəy*), which completes the rhyme with 時 *diəy* [Wang Niansun].

喜怒
Joy and anger,
取予
taking and giving,
人之謀也
underlie man’s schemes. (謀 *mjwəy*)

是故聖人
For this reason, the sage---

與時
In accordance with the times,
變
is ever changing,
而不化
but never transformed. (化 *xrwa*)

從物
In responding to things,
遷[^nei-ye-90]
he is ever flexible,
而不移
but never inconsistent. (移 *ria*)[^nei-ye-91]

[^nei-ye-90]: Inserting 遷 following 物 in accordance with the Yin commentary [Xu Weiyu].

[^nei-ye-91]: These two sentences are similar in content to the “Xin shu xia” 7a10 (67.12).

### [VI.3]

能正[^nei-ye-92]能靜
Capable of good judgment and remaining quiescent, (靜 *dzjieng*)

[^nei-ye-92]: 正. This refers to the above statement that the sage,
“in accordance with the times, is ever changing, but never transformed.
In responding to things, he is ever flexible, but never inconsistent.”

然後能定
He is consequently able to remain stable. (定 *deng*)

定心在中[^nei-ye-93]
There being a stable mind within, (中 *tiəwng*)

[^nei-ye-93]: Irregular rhyme: 中 (group 中)with 明 (group 暘), below.

耳目聰明
His ears and eyes are sharp and clear. (明 *miwang*)

四枝堅固
His four limbs strong and firm, (固 *kay*)

可以為精舍
He can serve the vital essence as a dwelling place. (舍 *st’jiay*)

精也者氣之精者也
The vital essence is the essence of the vital force.

氣通[^nei-ye-94]
When the vital force permeates,
乃生
there is life,
生乃思
and with life comes thought. (思 *sjiəy*)

[^nei-ye-94]: Reading 道 as 通 [Dai Wang].

思乃知
With thought comes knowledge,
知乃止矣
and with knowledge comes a stopping point. (止 *tjiəy*)
