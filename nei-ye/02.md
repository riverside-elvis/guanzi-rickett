## 內業 Nei ye II

### [II.1]

凡心之形[^nei-ye-67]
It is ever so that the mind’s gestalt (形 *geng*)

[^nei-ye-67]: Reading 刑 as 形 [Yasui].

自充自盈
Is naturally full and naturally replete, (盈 *yieng*)

自生自成
Naturally born and naturally perfected, (成 *djieng*)

其所以失[^nei-ye-68]
Should its function be impaired, (失 *st’jiet*)

[^nei-ye-68]: Deleting the 之 following 失 (*st’jiet* group 脂, 2nd tone),
in order to preserve the rhyme with 利 (*lier*, group 脂, 3rd tone),
“profit seeking” [Riegel/Haloun].

必以憂樂
It is certain to be due to sorrow and happiness,
喜怒[^nei-ye-69]
joy and anger,
欲利
desire and profit seeking. (利 *lier*)

[^nei-ye-69]: Riegel/Haloum would delete the four characters 憂樂窖怒,
“sorrow and happiness, joy and anger,” here and in the line below in order to preserve the meter.

能去憂樂
If we can rid ourselves of sorrow and happiness,
喜怒
joy and anger,
欲利
desire and profit seeking, (利 *lier*)

心乃反濟[^nei-ye-70]
The mind will revert to its flawless state. (濟 *tser*)

[^nei-ye-70]: Riegel/Haloun would emend 濟 (*tser*), “flawless state,” to 齊 (*dser*).
Thus: “the mind will return to a state of equilibrium (齊 *dser*).”

### [II.2]

彼心之情利安以寧
The mind’s inner reality is benefited by rest and quiet. (寧 *neng*)

勿煩勿亂
Avoid being harassed or confused,
和乃自成
and its harmony will naturally be complete. (成 *djieng*)

晳晳[^nei-ye-71]乎
How clear!
如在於側
As if right at our side. (側 *tsiek*)

[^nei-ye-71]: Emending 折折 to 晳晳 [Ding Shihan].

忽忽乎
How nebulous!
如將不得
As if beyond comprehension. (得 *tək*)

渺渺乎
How expansive!
如窮無極
As if exhausting the limitless. (極 *giək*)

此稽[^nei-ye-72]不遠
This inner reality, if we search for it, is never far
日用其德
and we may daily use its Power. (德 *tək*)

[^nei-ye-72]: Following the interpretation of Guo Moruo, Ma Feibai, and Zhao Shouzheng.
Zhang Peilun, Xu Weiyu, and Riegel/Haloun would emend 稽 to 道 so that the sentence would read:
“This Way is never far and we may daily use its Power.” Were this emendation adopted,
this and the preceding three lines would form the opening lines of stanza III, below.
