# 白心 Bai Xin (Purifying the Mind)

## [I]

以靖為宗[^bai-xin-6]
Take establishing tranquility as fundamental
以時為寶
and timeliness as a treasure. (寶 *pəw*)

[^bai-xin-6]: The first line of this paragraph, 建當立有, makes no sense.
He Ruzhang, Zhang Peilun, and Guo Moruo would emend 當 to 常 and 立 to 无 = 無
in accordance with _Zhuangzi_, X, 33/18b8-9 (Watson, _Chuang Tzu_, 372):
建之以常無有,主之以太一, “They established [standards in terms of] constant being and non-being
and took the Great Unity as their central concept.”
Thus it would read: “Establish constant standards in terms of non-being and being.”
However, concepts of non-being and being play no part in this chapter.
Furthermore such an emendation would make the rhyme pattern quite irregular.
Wang Niansun would emend 當 to 常 and 有 to 首 = 道, interpreting it to mean:
“Establish constant standards and set forth the Way.”
While this preserves the rhyme, it destroys the meter.
Therefore, I have followed Tao Hongqing and Xu Weiyu, who agree with Wang Niansun in emending
當 to 常 and 有 to 首 = 道, but, in accordance with the general sequence of ideas
as presented in the next paragraph, would reorder the lines of this paragraph to read:
以靖為宗，以時為寶，以正為儀，建常立道，和則能久.

以正[^bai-xin-7]為儀
Take what is correct for rules of deportment,
建常
establish constant standards,
立道
and set forth the Way. (道 *dəw*)

[^bai-xin-7]: Reading *zheng* 政 as *zheng* 正 [Wang Niansun].

和則能久
Then harmony will long prevail.

非吾儀
If something does not accord with my rules for proper deportment, (儀 *ngia*)

雖利
Even though beneficial to me,
不為
I will not act. (為 *yiwa*)

非吾常[^bai-xin-8]
If it does not accord with my constant standards, (常 *djang*)

[^bai-xin-8]: Emending 當 to 常 [Wang Niansun].

雖利
Even though beneficial to me,
不行
I will not promote it. (行 *grang*)

非吾道
If it does not accord with my way, (道 *dəw*)

雖利
Even though beneficial to me,
不取[^bai-xin-9]
I will not accept it. (取 *ts’jew*)

[^bai-xin-9]: Irregular rhyme: 取 (group 侯) with 道 (group 幽), above.

上之隨天
Above all, the sage follows Heaven, (天 *t’en*)

其次隨人
Only then does he follow other men. (人 *njien*)

人不倡[^bai-xin-10]
Unless others lead,
不和
he does not seek for harmony. (和 *gwa*)

[^bai-xin-10]: According to the _Huainanzi_, 14/12b 10-11:
“The sage is always behind and never to the fore;
always responds and never leads (唱 = 倡);
never advances, yet seeks; never retreats, yet yields.”

天不始
Unless Heaven initiates,
不隨
he does not seek to follow. (隋 *jywa*)

故其言也不廢
Therefore his words are never rejected. (廢 *pjwar*)

其事也不堕[^bai-xin-11]
Nor are his activities ever undermined. (堕 *t’wa*)

[^bai-xin-11]: Emending 隨 to 堕 [Wang Niansun and Igai].
Irregular rhyme: 堕 (group 歌) with 廢 (group 祭), above.
This is an uncommon pattern, but the meter appears to call for it.
