## [VIII]

置常立儀
By setting up constant standards and establishing rules for proper deportment,
能守貞[^bai-xin-49]乎
can one preserve those who are upright? (貞 *tieng*)

[^bai-xin-49]: Irregular rhyme: 貞 (group 耕) with 人 (group 真), below.
Zhang Wenhu and Xu Weiyu would emend 貞 (*tieng*) to 真 (*tjien*, group 真), “true,”
in order to preserve the rhyme with 人.

常事通道
By adhering to constant standards in undertakings and applying the way of good government everywhere,
能官人乎
can one govern others? (人 *njien*)

故書其惡者[^bai-xin-50]
Now, books preserve the worthless. (惡 *·ak*)

[^bai-xin-50]: This statement is reminiscent of the story in the _Zhuangzi_,
V, 13/18a 13-18b13 (Watson, _Chuang Tzu_, 152-153),
in which Wheelwright Pian criticizes Duke Huan of Qi for reading a book:
“When the men of old died they took with them things that couldn’t be handed down.
So what you are reading must be nothing but the chaff and dregs of the men of old.”

言其薄者
Words preserve the shallow. (薄 *bwak*)

上聖之人口無虛習也
The mouths of former sages never indulged in meaningless flapping, (習 *rjiep*)

手無虛指[^bai-xin-51]也
Nor did their hands indulge in the meaningless pointing of a brush. (指 *tjier*)

[^bai-xin-51]: Irregular rhyme: 指 (group 脂, 2nd tone) with 習 (group 緝, 4th tone), above.
This pattern, however, is rather uncommon.

物至
When matters arose,
而命之耳
they issued orders accordingly, and that is all.
發於名聲
What they articulated through name and sound
凝於體色
or conveyed through body gestures and facial expression
此其可諭者也
was what they were able to express.
不發於名聲
What they could not articulate through name and sound
不凝於體色
or convey throughbody gestures and facial expression
此其不可諭者也
was what they were unable to express.
及至於至者
For those who have achieved perfection,
教存可也
it does not matter whether their teachings survive
教亡可也
or not.
故曰
Therefore it is said:

濟於舟者
Whoever crosses water in a boat
和於水矣
is in harmony with the water; (水 *·st’jiwer*)

義於[^bai-xin-52]人者
Whoever fulfills his duties toward others
祥其鬼[^bai-xin-53]矣
is blessed by the spirits. (鬼 *kjwər*)

[^bai-xin-52]: Emending 其 to 於 in accordance with the Ancient, Liu, and Zhu editions
and the slightly different version of this quotation appearing earlier in the chapter,
9b3-4 (69.13) [Yasui and Wang Yinzhi].

[^bai-xin-53]: Emending 神 (*zdjien*) to 鬼 (*kjwər*) in accordance with
the the slightly different version of this quotation mentioned above [Wang Yinzhi and Guo Moruo].
鬼 (group 微) forms an irregular rhyme with 水 (group 脂).
